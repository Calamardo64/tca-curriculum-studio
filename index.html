    What this draft is designed to do

    Ten smaller steps make promotion more flexible. Each level is designed around ten topics that normally cover one term.

    The working target is roughly five new topics and five reinforcements, but this draft does not assign those labels automatically.

    Future tracks remain outside the ten-level grid: Advanced Class · White King and Masters Class · Black King.
